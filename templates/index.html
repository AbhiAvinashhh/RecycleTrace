The Problem

Segregation without verification is just sorting

Millions of citizens dutifully separate plastic, paper, and glass — but have no way to know if it ends up in a landfill anyway. Traditional recycling claims are largely unverifiable.

🤷

No Visibility

Once waste leaves your doorstep, there is zero tracking. The municipal chain becomes a black box.

📋

Unverified Claims

Facilities report theoretical numbers with zero independent review of scale receipts or photographic evidence.

⚖️

No Accountability

Missing quantities, mass discrepancy fraud, and bypassed recycling steps go completely undetected.

The Solution

A complete cryptographic chain of custody

Every waste batch receives a unique identifier and verifiable QR tag. Every handoff is signed by authorized operators and permanently recorded in MongoDB.

1

Register Waste

Select waste category and declared weight. Generate your tamper-resistant QR tracking label instantly.

2

Pickup & Weigh

Authorized collectors scan the QR code, log the physical scale weight, and record pickup custody in real-time.

3

Facility Processing

Receiving plants verify mass arrival, shred/pelletize, and submit quantitative yields alongside photo evidence.

4

Independent Audit

Auditors review weight tolerances, mass balances, and evidence before certifying the batch as Verified Recycled.

Rigorous Standards

We distinguish what others conflate

Most systems claim waste is "recycled" the moment a truck picks it up. RecycleTrace enforces a statutory distinction.

📦

Collected

A collector recorded pickup weight. The waste departed your home, but transformation has not begun.

🏭

Received at Facility

An authorized plant accepted physical custody and logged incoming weighbridge records.

📋

Processing Reported

The plant submitted operational metrics with declared recyclable yield and residual outputs.

✓

Verified Recycled

An auditor reviewed mathematical mass reconciliation and photographic proofs. Genuine certified proof.

Role-Based Governance

Four roles, one transparent ecosystem

🏠

Citizens

Register segregated waste, download QR codes, follow live event feeds, and view certified recycling audits.

🚛

Collectors

Scan batch QR tags in the field, record verified weigh-ins, and log inter-facility transfer events.

🏭

Facility Operators

Receive shipments, document processing batches, compute yields, and attach verifiable photo evidence.

🔍

Auditors & Admins

Investigate discrepancies, review photographic evidence, approve recycling certifications, and inspect MongoDB analytics.
