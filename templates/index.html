The Problem

Segregation without verification is just sorting

Millions of citizens dutifully separate plastic, paper, and glass — but have no way to know if it ends up in a landfill anyway. Recycling claims are largely unverifiable.

🤷

No Visibility

Once waste leaves your doorstep, there's no tracking. It could go anywhere.

📋

Unverified Claims

Facilities report recycling numbers with no independent review of evidence.

⚖️

No Accountability

Missing weight, quantity discrepancies, and skipped stages go unnoticed.

The Solution

A complete chain of custody for your waste

Every batch gets a unique ID and QR code. Every transfer is recorded by an authorized operator. Every recycling claim is reviewed before it becomes verified.

1
📝

Register Your Waste

Select category, enter estimated weight, and get a unique batch ID and QR code.

2
🚛

Collector Confirms Pickup

An authorized collector records the actual weight and confirms transport to a facility.

3
🏭

Facility Processes & Reports

The recycling facility records input, recycled output, rejected, and residual weights with evidence.

4
✅

Auditor Verifies

An independent reviewer checks evidence and weight reconciliation before marking anything as verified.

What Verified Means

We distinguish what others conflate

📦

Collected

A collector confirmed pickup and recorded weight. The waste left your home.

🏭

Received at Facility

A verified facility confirmed receipt. Weight at arrival is on record.

📋

Processing Reported

The facility submitted a processing report with claimed output quantities.

✓

Verified Recycled

An auditor reviewed the evidence and weight reconciliation. This is actual proof.

Only this status means recycled.
Who's Involved

Four roles, one transparent system

🏠

Citizens

Register waste, track batches, download QR codes, view verified outcomes.

🚛

Collectors

Confirm pickup, record weights, transfer to authorized facilities.

🏭

Facility Operators

Receive batches, record processing, upload evidence, submit reports.

🔍

Auditors / Admins

Review evidence, approve or reject reports, flag discrepancies, manage the platform.
